🧱 1. Bills (the core category)
This is your main table for defining types of expenses (e.g., rent, food, subscriptions).
Other entities like OutFlow, CurrentMonth, and BudgetAndActual reference this.
Think of it as your "category manager."

💸 2. OutFlow (tracks actual spending)
Each record = one spending entry (e.g., “Paid ₱1500 for electricity”).
Linked to Bills → tells you what bill the money was spent on.

✅ How it works with Bills:
You can query:
👉 "Show me all spending under 'Food'" → use the bill.id reference.

💰 3. InFlow (tracks incoming money)
Actor person   ← 🔗 many-to-one
Represents income transactions (e.g., salary, side hustle, etc.)
Not directly tied to Bills, but is part of your overall balance logic.

📆 4. CurrentMonth (planned monthly budgets)
Tracks the budgeted amount for a bill in the current month.
Used for comparing actual spending (from OutFlow) against monthly expectations.
✅ Works with:
Bills → to know what you're budgeting for.
Your app can use this for monthly budget planning screens.

📊 5. BudgetAndActual (comparison data)
This is a summary table used to compare:
What was budgeted (budget)
What was actually spent (actualOutFlow)
The difference
✅ Generated from:
CurrentMonth (for budget)
OutFlow (for actuals)
Use this for monthly budget performance reports.

🧮 6. Balance (what's left)
Just tracks the remaining money (e.g., wallet or account balance).
Likely updated whenever an InFlow or OutFlow is recorded.
